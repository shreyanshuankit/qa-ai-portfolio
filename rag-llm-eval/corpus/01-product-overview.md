# Northbloom CarryMug — product overview
FICTIONAL PRODUCT. Portfolio sample only.

The Northbloom CarryMug is a 420 ml self-heating travel mug with a magnetic lid
and an app-free NFC heat-preset chip built into the base.

It ships in three colours: slate, oat, and cedar. The empty mug weighs 310 grams.
The inner vessel is brushed stainless steel; the outer sleeve is matte recycled
aluminium. A fill line is etched at 400 ml so liquid does not reach the lid seal
when the mug is heated.

Northbloom is a fictional company and this mug is not sold anywhere.
