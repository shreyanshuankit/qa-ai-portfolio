# Shipping and returns
FICTIONAL PRODUCT. Portfolio sample only.

In this fictional policy, unused mugs may be returned within 30 days of delivery
for a full refund, provided the heater has not been registered as activated for
more than two heat cycles.

Orders in the made-up Cedar Vale region ship in 3 to 5 business days. Expedited
overnight shipping is not offered. Replacement lids ship separately and are not
covered by the 30-day mug return window once opened.
