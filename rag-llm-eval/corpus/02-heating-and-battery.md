# Heating and battery
FICTIONAL PRODUCT. Portfolio sample only.

The CarryMug has three heat presets: 40 °C (warm), 55 °C (hot), and 70 °C (hottest).
Presets are selected by tapping an NFC card or phone to the base; there is no
on-mug display of the numeric temperature, only three LED marks.

A full battery holds heat at the 55 °C preset for up to 3 hours, or at the 70 °C
preset for up to 90 minutes, in a 20 °C room. The 40 °C preset lasts up to 5 hours.
The mug does not actively cool a drink; it only holds or raises temperature.
