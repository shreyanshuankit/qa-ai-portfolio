# Safety and warranty
FICTIONAL PRODUCT. Portfolio sample only.

The CarryMug must not be put in a microwave, oven, or freezer. It is not a toy
and is not intended for children under 3 years old.

The fictional warranty is 18 months from the date of purchase and covers the
heater, battery, and lid magnet against manufacturing defects. It does not cover
drops, dishwasher damage to the vessel body, or use with a third-party lid.
