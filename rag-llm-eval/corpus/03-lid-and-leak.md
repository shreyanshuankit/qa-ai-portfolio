# Lid and leak resistance
FICTIONAL PRODUCT. Portfolio sample only.

The lid is magnetic and seats with an audible click. When the lid is fully seated,
the mug is rated leak-resistant at any angle for 30 seconds and is suitable for a
bag if the lid has clicked shut.

The lid is top-rack dishwasher safe. The vessel body must be hand washed; it is
not dishwasher safe. A worn lid gasket should be replaced rather than forced.
