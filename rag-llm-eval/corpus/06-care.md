# Care and cleaning
FICTIONAL PRODUCT. Portfolio sample only.

Hand-wash the vessel body with mild soap and a soft bottle brush. The lid may be
washed on the top rack of a dishwasher. Do not use bleach, steel wool, or
abrasive powders.

If mineral film builds up, descale with a 1:3 white-vinegar-to-water soak for
20 minutes, then rinse twice. Do not leave the mug soaking overnight.
